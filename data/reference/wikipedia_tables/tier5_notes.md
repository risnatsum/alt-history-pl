# Tier 5 (Conference / National League) table notes, 2008–09 to 2025–26

Source: English Wikipedia season pages, read via WebFetch (which returns a model-summarised view of the page, so "quotes" below are as WebFetch reported them). Club names in tier5.csv have the (C)/(P)/(O)/(R)/(E)/(D) markers removed. Every page was read; none failed.

Unless stated otherwise, every season's table says: "Rules for classification: 1) points; 2) goal difference; 3) number of goals scored" (from 2016–17 the list continues "4) Number of matches won; 5) Head-to-head results").

- 2008: Table notes section is empty; deductions are only evidenced by reference titles: "Five-point deduction for Oxford" (BBC Sport, 12 January 2009), "Crawley win FA appeal over points" (BBC Sport, 27 February 2009), "Mansfield hit by points deduction" (BBC Sport, 6 October 2008). The page does not state their sizes; printed points imply Oxford United −5, Crawley Town −1, Mansfield Town −4 (my arithmetic, not page text).
- 2008: Altrincham "remain in the Conference after a third consecutive reprieve from relegation, following Halifax Town's demotion for financial reasons."
- 2009: Salisbury City were deducted 10 points for entering administration; demoted to the Southern League at season end for failing to come out of administration (marked D, R).
- 2009: Gateshead were deducted one point for failing to fulfil a fixture.
- 2009: Grays Athletic were docked two points for fielding ineligible players; relegated but declined to join the Conference South for financial reasons; later accepted into the Isthmian League Division One North.
- 2009: Chester City were deducted 25 points for entering administration and insolvency; expelled on 26 February 2010; record expunged on 8 March 2010 (at expulsion: P28 W5 D7 L16 GF23 GA42 Pts −3). Shown in the table as 24th with all zeros (in the CSV as 0s, as printed).
- 2009: Forest Green Rovers were reprieved from relegation. The bottom four were scheduled for relegation; because two clubs were expelled, only the bottom two (Ebbsfleet United and Grays Athletic) were relegated. Division finished with 23 active clubs, 44 games each.
- 2010: "Kidderminster Harriers and Histon were docked five points for submitting misleading financial information."
- 2010: "Rushden & Diamonds were docked five points for submitting misleading financial information"; expelled from the Football Conference on 11 June 2011.
- 2010: Kettering Town were docked two points for fielding an ineligible player.
- 2010: Southport were reprieved from relegation.
- 2011: "Darlington were deducted ten points for entering administration." "At the end of the season the club folded, and reformed as Darlington 1883 in the 2012–13 Northern League Division One."
- 2011: "Kettering Town were deducted three points for failing to pay football creditors." "At the end of the season they resigned from the Football Conference, dropping down two divisions."
- 2012: No deduction, expulsion or reprieve notes for the Conference Premier table (deduction notes on the page belong to North/South tables).
- 2013: "Alfreton Town were deducted three points for fielding an ineligible player."
- 2013: "Aldershot Town were deducted ten points for entering administration."
- 2013: "On 10 June 2014, Hereford United were expelled from the Conference due to financial irregularities." Chester were reprieved from relegation as a result.
- 2013: "On 13 June 2014, Salisbury City were demoted to the Conference South due to financial irregularities." Dartford were reprieved from relegation as a result. Salisbury were "expelled from the Football Conference altogether" on 4 July 2014.
- 2014: Forest Green Rovers "were deducted three points for fielding an ineligible player" (win over Southport, 9 August 2014).
- 2014: AFC Telford United "were deducted three points for fielding an ineligible player" (win over Lincoln City, 11 April 2015).
- 2014: Nuneaton Town "were deducted three points for fielding an ineligible player" (win over Welling United, 28 February 2015).
- 2015: No deduction/expulsion/reprieve notes for the top-division table.
- 2016: No deduction/expulsion/reprieve notes for the top-division table ("Notes:" heading is empty).
- 2017: No deduction/expulsion/reprieve notes for the top-division table.
- 2018: "Gateshead were deducted 9 points for breach of financial regulations."
- 2018: "Gateshead were demoted to the National League North on 8 June 2019 because of financial irregularities. As a result, Aldershot Town were given a reprieve from relegation."
- 2018: "Braintree Town were deducted 3 points for fielding an ineligible player in a game against AFC Fylde."
- 2019: Season ended early on 22 April 2020 (COVID-19). On 17 June 2020 the final tables were decided on a points-per-game basis; Barrow named champions. Clubs played unequal numbers of games (35–39); table order follows PPG (printed PPG: Barrow 1.89 ... Chorley 0.68), not pts.
- 2019: Footnote on Maidenhead United: "As a result of the knock-on effects of Bury's expulsion from League One, the fourth bottom team was reprieved from relegation for numerical reasons." Only three clubs (Ebbsfleet United, AFC Fylde, Chorley) were relegated.
- 2019: AFC Fylde considered a legal challenge to their relegation, with support from Ebbsfleet United, but did not proceed. No deductions or expunged records noted.
- 2020: Dover Athletic (23rd, shown as all zeros): "Results expunged"; Dover "refused to play matches due to a lack of promised funding" and "all results would be expunged"; club "deducted 12 points for the 2021–22 season."
- 2020: Macclesfield Town (24th, all zeros): "Club expelled and folded"; were "deducted 17 points for entering into an insolvency event the previous season"; "no records were expunged" because it folded before the season started.
- 2020: "due to the cancellation of both the National North and South seasons, there was no relegation from the National League in the 2020-21 season." King's Lynn Town marked "Reprieved from relegation." 22 clubs completed 42 games.
- 2021: 23-club division (Macclesfield folding and knock-on effects of Bury's collapse). No table note explains Dover Athletic's deduction; only a reference title: "Dover Athletic: National League club hit with fine, points deduction and results expunged" (BBC Sport, 26 March 2021). Printed 1 pt from W2 D7 implies −12 (matches the 2020–21 page text above).
