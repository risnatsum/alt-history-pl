# Parachute payment rules by season (all amounts £; parachute.csv amounts are £m)

Quotes are as returned by WebFetch (a summarising model; quotes capped ~125 chars). Season = start year (2009 = 2009/10).

## 2006/07 - c.2009/10: two-year scheme
- Wikipedia: parachute payments began 2006-07, relegated clubs got "half of the per-club Premier League basic TV money for two seasons". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League_parachute_and_solidarity_payments
- CSB Liverpool (Evans et al., Sep 2025): "Initially, the payments were 55% followed by 45% of this amount for the respective seasons." https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/media/livacuk/schoolofmanagement/centreforsportsbusiness/CSB,policy,briefing,-,parachute,payments.pdf
- Actual 2009/10: "£12.4m to each of them in 2009-10" (both year-1 and year-2 clubs equal, ~50% of £24.7m equal TV share) - supports Wikipedia's "half for two seasons" rather than 55/45. https://sportingintelligence832.substack.com/p/the-premier-league-where-the-money-went-in-2009-10

## Third and fourth years added (c.2009/10 - 2010/11)
- CSB: "From the 2009/10 season the relegated clubs received a payment of 20% of the amount of the equal share for a third season." / "In the following season this was extended to a fourth season with 25% of the amount of the equal share" (3rd and 4th). Same in Evans 2024 (Birkbeck): https://eprints.bbk.ac.uk/id/eprint/53557/1/53557.pdf
- Evans Table 1 (club counts by season ending): first year-3 recipient in season ending 2012, first year-4 in season ending 2014 -> i.e. clubs relegated in 2009 got a 3rd year, clubs relegated 2010+ got 4 years.
- Sportingintelligence, 4 Aug 2010: parachute to "increase from £16m per year for two years to £48m spread over four years". https://sportingintelligence832.substack.com/p/good-news-for-blackpool-premier-league-confirms-increased-parachute-cash-up-to-48m-and-372m-to-be-handed-to-football-league-040804
- Swiss Ramble (May 2012): "teams now will receive £48 million (£16 million in each of the first two years, and £8 million in years three and four)". http://swissramble.blogspot.com/2012/05/leeds-united-marching-on-together.html
- 2013-16 PL TV cycle: Swiss Ramble (Aug 2013): "increase by 23% from £48 million to £59 million (over four years)", "£23 million in year one, £18 million in year two, then £9 million in years three and four". http://swissramble.blogspot.com/2013/08/championship-finances-201112-numbers.html  Later (Jun 2015): "£25 million in year 1; £20 million in year 2; and £10 million in each of years 3 and 4" (£65m). http://swissramble.blogspot.com/2015/06/fulham-penthouse-and-pavement.html
- Swiss Ramble thread (2018) labels pre-2016/17 as Y1 55% / Y2 45% / Y3 25% / Y4 25%, gross figures less £2.3m solidarity-equivalent deduction. https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1031811168132841473.html

## 2016/17 onward: three years, 55/45/20, one-season rule
- Sky Sports, 3 June 2015: promoted clubs relegated after one season "will no longer benefit from full parachute payments from the 2016/17 season"; payments move from four years to three; clubs relegated after one season "will only get the first two years of payments rather than the full three years"; 55% / 45% / 20% of an equal broadcast share. Hull, QPR, Burnley (relegated 2015) not affected (£24m, £19.3m, £9.6m, £9.6m). https://www.skysports.com/football/news/9873622/parachute-payments-overhauled-for-promoted-premier-league-clubs
- CSB: "from the 2016/17 season the fourth season payment was withdrawn, the third season payment reverted to 20%" and "a rule was introduced limiting payments to clubs that returned to the Premier League for a single season."
- Wikipedia: system "applies to clubs relegated from 2015–16 onward" (i.e. relegated at the END of 2015/16); earlier system "spread over 4 years". The 20% third year only if club was in PL for more than one season before relegation. Parachutes stop on promotion.
- **Exact first season**: the new rules apply to clubs relegated at the end of 2015/16 (Newcastle, Norwich, Aston Villa), whose first parachute season was 2016/17. Clubs relegated in 2013, 2014 and 2015 kept the old 4-year entitlement at uplifted values: QPR (rel. 2015) got year 3 £16.6m in 2017/18 and year 4 £17.0m in 2018/19; Reading/Wigan (rel. 2013) got a year-4 payment of £16.3m in 2016/17 (Wikipedia table; sportingintelligence 2017-18). First club denied a 3rd year under the one-season rule: Norwich (rel. 2016 after one season) - Evans Table 1 shows only one year-3 recipient (Villa) in 2018/19 (Wikipedia's table shows Norwich 43.1 for 2018/19, which looks like an error).
- Sportingintelligence 2016-17 (1 Jun 2017): relegated clubs "get £41m each this time"; equal share "just over £79m" (includes central commercial; parachute % is applied to broadcast equal share of £74.4m = 35.3 domestic + 39.1 overseas per Swiss Ramble thread, giving 55.0%).
- Price of Football (May 2018) cites PL rule D.25 for Sunderland: year 1 2017/18 £41m, year 2 2018/19 £33.5m, year 3 2019/20 £14.9m (projection). https://priceoffootball.com/sunderland-short-changed/
- EFL evidence (June 2025): 2024/25 Year One £48.9m, Year Two £40.1m, Year Three £17.8m. https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5901/cmpublic/FootballGovernance/memo/FGB03.htm ; (May 2024): 2022/23 £47.6m / £38.9m / £17.3m. https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5804/cmpublic/FootballGovernance/memo/FGB05.htm
- From 2018/19 PL stopped publishing the club breakdown (eflanalysis / Wikipedia "gulf" page).

## Solidarity basis (for reference)
- 2006/07: £1m per Championship club (Wikipedia).
- 2010/11-2012/13: £2.2m Champ / £335,000 L1 / £225,000 L2 per season (sportingintelligence Aug 2010).
- to 2015/16: £2.3m / £360,000 / £240,000 (Sky Jun 2015).
- From 2016/17: percentage of a year-3 parachute payment: 30% Championship, 4.5% League One, 3% League Two (Sky; sportingintelligence; Wikipedia). Clubs receiving parachutes do not get solidarity (Swiss Ramble).

## EFL's own parachute (not PL) from 2016/17 (Wikipedia)
- Relegated from Championship: 11.1% of Championship Basic Award, one season; from League One: 12.6% of L1 Basic Award, one season; from League Two: 100% of L2 Basic Award year 1, 50% year 2.
