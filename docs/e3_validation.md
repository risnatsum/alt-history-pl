# E3 validation report

Generated by `python -m altpl.build_e3`. Do not edit by hand. Every simulated number is a model estimate.

10,000 runs per test, fixed seeds; 32 minutes. Full tables in `data/processed/e3_backtest.xlsx`; the standard output workbook for the knock-on run is `data/processed/e3_knock_on_example.xlsx`.

## 1. Reproducing reality

Reality simulated with every real result kept (200 runs, 2008/09 to 2025/26): every final table matches reality (2,083 club-seasons, points and positions; tied clubs may swap), and every run's divisions match real membership in every season, including the expulsions, demotions, reprieves and the 2019/20 points-per-game seasons. Play-offs reproduce through the real winners.

## 2 and 3. Re-simulating single seasons

Each season replayed with its real clubs. *Fully simulated*: every fixture drawn from the season's own ratings. *A fifth hidden*: 9,217 of 46,036 real matches (20%) hidden, ratings refitted without them, the hidden matches simulated and the rest kept. Brier skill compares each probability with a flat guess at the division's base rate (1 = perfect, 0 = no better).

Fully simulated seasons are cautious: real points fall outside the 5-95% range less often than the 10% they should, and confident predictions come true more often than stated. E2's in-sample back-test found the same (95-98% inside): the ratings lean towards the bookmakers' view, which is less extreme than final tables. With a fifth of fixtures hidden, the situation scenarios are in, the ranges and chances are much closer to right.

The step-up penalty and money boost are in strength units (attack + defence), split equally between attack and defence; the step-up penalty is per division above the club's real one, the money boost applies only to clubs promoted in the run but not in reality. Both are off by default.
