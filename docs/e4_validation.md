# E4 validation report

Generated by `python -m altpl.build_e4`. Do not edit by hand. Every model number is an estimate, not a fact.

Full tables: `data/processed/e4_backtest.xlsx` (price list, checks) and `data/processed/e4_finance_example.xlsx` (standard outputs plus finance sheets for the example run, 10,000 runs). 10 minutes.

## How the model works

For every run, season and club the model prices where the club played and finished, then subtracts what the same model gives for where it really played and finished. Components: PL central payments by position (base payment, merit per place, facility-fee slope), parachute payments under the rules of the season the club went down, PL solidarity payments, the EFL's own central distribution, UEFA prize money for European places earned through the PL table, and matchday plus commercial revenue gained per division climbed. Nominal pounds of the season paid, and 2025/26 pounds with the ONS CPI.
